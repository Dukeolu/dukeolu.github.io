[I am a data analyst with experience in using various tools and technologies to collect, organize, and analyze data to inform business decisions. I am proficient in Python, R, SQL, Excel, Power BI, and Tableau, and have a solid understanding of data analytics techniques such as dashboard building, report writing, data merging, statistics, and critical thinking.]

Every case study on this site follows the same discipline: start from a real business question, not a dataset looking for a use — use the right tool for each step rather than forcing everything through one (SQL to segment and rank, Python where a model or a cross-check earns its keep, Excel where a stakeholder needs to open the file and trust the formula) — and end every analysis with a specific, costed recommendation, not just a chart. Where a finding doesn't hold up (an AUC that's honestly modest, a factor that turns out to be a confound, not a driver) that's stated plainly rather than smoothed over.

The underlying datasets are simulated rather than downloaded — each one is built with realistic structure and a deliberately seeded, discoverable mechanism, disclosed in every case's README, so the method is the transferable part.
