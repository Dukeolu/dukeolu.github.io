Case 03 · Finance & Budgeting

Finding where margin is quietly leaking

ExcelSQLPythonDashboard

The business is profitable overall, but budget-to-actual reviews keep coming in over on cost-to-serve in specific regions and categories — and until now, nobody had broken out where. Over the trailing 24 months, actual profit came in $332,657 below budget. Small relative to $35.5M in budgeted profit (-0.9%), but the finance team's instinct that “something's off in a couple of areas” turns out to be exactly right.

Approach

  1. 01

    Excel — A live-formula workbook (6,517 formulas, zero errors on recalc): a three-part profit-variance bridge — volume, discount, COGS — computed per row and proven to sum exactly to the total variance, plus a conditional-formatting heatmap that makes the leak clusters visually obvious.

  2. 02

    SQL — Rank all 24 region×category combos by total variance with window functions, including a running cumulative share of the total leak.

  3. 03

    Python — Reproduce the same volume/discount/COGS decomposition (cross-checked to the dollar against the Excel bridge), then run the recommendation as a counterfactual: what comes back if the worst combos simply held budgeted rates?

  4. 04

    Dashboard — Two separately-scaled charts — totals and effects — rather than one chart spanning $0–$37M that would hide the effects entirely.

Key finding

The shortfall is not a demand problem — the volume/revenue effect is actually slightly positive (+$38,622). It's almost entirely a discount and cost-discipline story: discount effect -$68,137, COGS effect -$303,142. And it's sharply concentrated: 4 of the 24 region×category combos account for 84% of the entire leak — Electronics Accessories in the West and Northeast (a ramping supplier cost overrun), and Outdoor & Sporting in the South and Midwest (discount creep in two competitive regions). The Electronics Accessories/West trend was still getting worse as of the most recent month in the data, not a one-month blip.

Recommendation & business impact

Don't launch a network-wide cost-cutting initiative — that solves a problem four combos actually have. Fix the four: renegotiate or re-source the Electronics Accessories supply chain driving the cost overrun, and reinstate discount discipline in Outdoor & Sporting. Counterfactual profit if those four combos had held budgeted rates: $35,555,035 over 24 months — $384,278 recovered, or roughly $192,139/year annualized.

The pitch in one line: The business isn't leaking money everywhere — it's leaking from four specific taps, and turning those off recovers roughly $192K a year without touching anything that's actually working.
